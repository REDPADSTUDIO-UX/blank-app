1 EXT - BARREN / SNOWY LANDSCAPE - DUSK (INTRO)

Swirls of snow obscure the rocky formations of a mountain
ridge. A bleak and barren landscape moves under us. In the
distance a half-dozen figures zigzag through the snow.

Five ragged men attack a young girl, SINTEL, who brandishes
a spiked spear. She spins and parries, whips around and
knocks a man into the snow. Panting, she grabs his weapon -
a double-bladed staff.

Someone slashes at her leg and Sintel twists in pain,
roaring. Sintel lashes out venomously. Before long all
five bandits lie motionless at her feet.

Sintel staggers forward, breathing hard. Her face betrays
her exhaustion; she limps on into the biting cold.

                    SHAMAN (V.O.)
          You’re lucky to be alive. Many
          strong men have fallen to the
          gatekeepers.

Finally she collapses into the snow, her eyes shut tight.

BLACK.

                    SHAMAN (V.O.) (cont’d)
          Here, take a sip.


2 INT - DARK SHAMAN’S HUT - DUSK (INTRO)

Blinking through watery eyes, SINTEL sips the steaming broth
from a copper dish. The wrinkled face of a SHAMAN examines
her from the shadows.

                    SHAMAN
          What brings you to the edge of the
          world?

Sintel stares into the broth numbly.

                    SINTEL
          I am searching for someone I love.

                    SHAMAN
          A sibling, perhaps? A lover?

                    SINTEL
               (looking up)
          A dragon.

SLAM CUT to BLACK.
