# ঘর ১:১৭ — Episode 1 production bible

Original supernatural family mystery for Nishikotha. No adaptation licence from Humayun Ahmed's rights holders is in evidence. No protected story, characters or dialogue used. The 30-episode architecture is in season_outline.md; Episode 1 is expanded to 300 seconds.

## Format and honest provenance
16:9, 1280×720; CPU-authored 2D articulated puppet animation at 12 fps. Optional Wan2.2 clips may enhance specific shots only when successfully returned and verified. This is a stylized animated drama, not photorealistic actors, not motion capture, and not a claim of perfect phoneme lip-sync. Speech amplitude drives mouth opening; eye blinks, gaze, head movement, arm gestures, walk movement, rotating tape reels, rain and spreading water have their own animation tracks. Titles and subtitles are editorial graphics.

The same draw rig, palette, proportions and hair geometry are reused across every CPU shot. Neural enhancement uses the same actual frame as its visual reference; reject identity-changing results rather than claim continuity.

## Recurring locations
Rafi's inherited house: rain window on left, late father's portrait near middle, ochre desk under the window, locked panel door on the right. Tape recorder never changes design. Warm desk practical against desaturated blue night. Floorboards converge to centre. Hazera leaves before the supernatural event.
Mina's apartment: blue night window on left, bookcase right, muted lavender seating; crosscuts establish that she is elsewhere, not inside the locked room.

## Sound / performance
No continuous narrator. Dialogue belongs to characters. Play discomfort through pauses, eye motion and small gestures. Early warmth about tea and food precedes unease. Rafi does not shout every line. Consumer Edge speech is synthetic and has limited emotional control; no Azure Speech API, account, key or billing.
An original procedural score and practical cue approximations are mixed below dialogue. Tape echo remains intelligible. Burned Bangla captions plus selectable SRT track aid comprehension. Synthetic foley is a limitation, not field-recorded sound.

## Timeline / continuity
